# Workout Logbook & Progression Tracker

4-day Upper / Lower routine with Dynamic Double Progression (DDP).

---

## Progression Rules

1. **Set 1 dictates load changes**: If Set 1 reaches the top of the rep bracket at the target RIR, increase load by 2.5% to 5% for that exercise next week.
2. **Fatigue drop-off**: If fatigue drops reps below the bracket floor on Sets 2 or 3 (e.g., fewer than 6 reps), reduce load by 5% to 10% on the next set.
3. **Proximity to failure**:
   - Axial compounds (RDL, DB Press, Hack Squat): 1–3 RIR. Do not train to muscular failure.
   - Machines and isolations: 0–1 RIR. Final sets can use prescribed extenders to failure.
