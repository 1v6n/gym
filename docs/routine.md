# 4-Day Upper / Lower Hypertrophy Routine

A 4-day hypertrophy program for natural lifters. Uses Dynamic Double Progression (DDP), stretch-loaded exercise variations, and fatigue drop-offs to regulate training load.

---

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Workout | Movement Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Monday | Upper A | Horizontal Push, Vertical Pull, Arms |
| Tuesday | Lower A | Quad Bias, Knee Flexion, Calves |
| Wednesday | Rest | Recovery |
| Thursday | Upper B | Incline/Vertical Push, Horizontal Pull, Rear Delts |
| Friday | Lower B | Posterior Chain (Hip Hinge), Adductors, Calves |
| Saturday | Rest | Recovery |
| Sunday | Rest | Recovery |

## Session Templates

### Upper A (Horizontal Push and Vertical Pull)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Target RIR | Rest | Execution & Technique Notes |
| :-: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Flat Dumbbell Press | 3 × 6–8 | 2 RIR | 3.0 min | Progression driver. Deep stretch at bottom; keep wrists stacked over elbows. |
| 2 | Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown | 3 × 8–10 | 1–2 RIR | 2.0 min | Full overhead stretch with 2-second pause at top before pulling. |
| 3 | Incline Smith Machine Press (30°) | 3 × 8–10 | 1–2 RIR | 2.0 min | 3-second eccentric. Touch clavicular line. |
| 4 | Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row | 3 × 10–12 | 1 RIR | 90 sec | Incline bench at 30–45°. Flare elbows ~45° for upper back. |
| 5 | Cable Lateral Raise | 3 × 12–15 | 0–1 RIR | 60–90 sec | Pulley at wrist height. Final set rest-pause: reach 0 RIR, rest 15s, 4 reps, rest 15s, 3 reps. |
| 6 | Overhead Cable "Katana" Extension | 2 × 10–12 | 0–1 RIR | 90 sec | Cables crossed behind head for long-head stretch. Final set: 4–6 bottom-half partials at failure. |
| 7 | Incline Dumbbell Curl (45°) | 2 × 10–12 | 0–1 RIR | 90 sec | Shoulders pinned back behind torso. Final set: 4–5 deep stretch partials at failure. |

Exercises 6 and 7 can run as an antagonist superset (Triceps -> 60s rest -> Biceps -> 60s rest) without reducing force output.

| :-: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |

| 3 | Bulgarian Split Squat | 2 × 8–10 / leg | 2 RIR | 90 sec / leg | Slight forward torso lean to work glutes and quads evenly. |
| 4 | Leg Extension Machine | 2 × 12–15 | 0 RIR | 90 sec | Controlled eccentric. Final set rest-pause: reach 0 RIR, rest 15s, 4 reps, rest 15s, 3 reps. |
| 5 | Standing Calf Raise | 3 × 8–10 | 0–1 RIR | 90 sec | Dead-stop 2-second pause in full dorsiflexion on every rep to remove tendon bounce. |

## Progression Rules: Dynamic Double Progression (DDP)

1. **Top Set Decides the Increase**:
   - Keep load constant on Set 1 until you reach the top of the rep bracket (e.g., 8 reps in a 6–8 bracket) at the prescribed RIR.
   - When achieved, add 2.5% to 5% load to Set 1 the following week.

2. **Fatigue Drop-off on Subsequent Sets**:
   - If fatigue drops your reps below the bracket floor on Sets 2 or 3 (e.g., fewer than 6 reps on a 6–8 bracket), drop load by 5% to 10% on the next set to keep repetitions in target range.

3. **Proximity to Failure (RIR Guardrails)**:
   - **Axial compounds (RDL, DB Press, Hack Squat)**: Keep 1–3 RIR. Do not train to muscular failure on axial lifts.
   - **Machines and isolations**: Train to 0–1 RIR. Failure is safe on final sets with prescribed extenders.

---

## Deload Protocol

- **Frequency**: Every 6 to 10 weeks, or when performance drops across 2 consecutive sessions alongside joint ache or fatigue.
- **Duration**: 1 week.
- **Adjustments**:
  - **Load**: Keep current working weights.
  - **Volume**: Cut total sets by 50% (3 sets become 1–2 sets; 2 sets become 1 set).
  - **Intensity**: Train at 3–4 RIR across all movements.
  - **Extenders**: Skip rest-pause, lengthened partials, and loaded stretches.
