Overload Engine (DDP)

1. Dynamic Double Progression

Set 1 drives weight progression. Keep the weight the same until Set 1 hits the ceiling (e.g., 8 reps in a 6 to 8 target) at the prescribed RIR. Once you hit the ceiling, increase the load by 2.5% to 5% next week.

2. Intra-Session Regulation

If fatigue drops Set 2 or Set 3 below the floor (fewer than 6 reps), reduce the load by 5% to 10% on that set to stay in the target hypertrophy bracket.

3. RIR & Joint Safety

Axial lifts (hack squats, RDLs, dumbbell presses): stay between 1 and 3 RIR. Do not train to concentric failure. Isolation machines and cables: 0 to 1 RIR.

4. Last-Set Intensity Techniques

  • Rest-pause: At 0 RIR, rack the weight for 15 seconds, complete 4 reps, rest 15 seconds, and complete 3 final reps.
  • Lengthened partials: When full-range reps fail, complete 4 to 6 pulses in the deepest, most stretched half of the movement.
  • Loaded stretch: Hold the fully lengthened position under load for 10 seconds.
