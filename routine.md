# Evidence-Based 4-Day Upper / Lower Hypertrophy Routine

An evidence-based hypertrophy program designed for intermediate natural lifters, prioritizing stretch-mediated mechanics, fractional volume titration, and Dynamic Double Progression (DDP).

---

| Day | Workout | Focus / Movement Biases |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monday** | **Upper A** | Horizontal Push, Vertical Pull, Direct Arms |

| **Wednesday** | **Rest** | Complete Recovery (Cardio / Mobility optional) |
| **Thursday** | **Upper B** | Vertical / Incline Push, Horizontal Pull, Rear Delts |

| 5 | **Cable Lateral Raise** | 3 × 12–15 | 0–1 RIR | 60–90 sec | Set pulley at wrist height. **Final Set Rest-Pause:** reach 0 RIR, rest 15s, perform 4 reps, rest 15s, perform 3 reps. |

## 📈 Progression Engine: Dynamic Double Progression (DDP)

1. **Top Set Dictates the Increase:**
   - Focus all progressive overload attention on **Set 1**.
   - Stay at the same load until **Set 1 hits the ceiling** of the rep bracket (e.g., exactly 8 reps in a 6–8 bracket) while strictly adhering to the assigned RIR.
   - Once achieved, add **2.5% to 5% load** to Set 1 in the following week.

2. **Intra-Session Back-Off Management:**
   - If accumulated fatigue causes subsequent sets (Sets 2 or 3) to drop below the rep bracket floor (e.g., falling below 6 reps), reduce the load on that set by **5% to 10%** to keep your repetitions inside the optimal hypertrophy zone.

3. **Proximity to Failure (RIR Guardrails):**
   - **Axial Compounds (RDL, DB Press, Hack Squat):** Maintain **1–3 RIR**. Never train to muscular failure or form breakdown on these lifts.
   - **Stable Machines & Isolations:** Train to **0–1 RIR**. Muscular failure is safe and encouraged on final sets with prescribed intensity extenders.

---

## 🔄 Deload Protocol

* **Frequency:** Every **6 to 10 weeks**, or triggered when performance regresses across 2 consecutive workouts alongside elevated joint ache or systemic lethargy.
* **Duration:** 1 week.
* **Parameters:**
  * **Load:** Keep your current working weights unchanged.
  * **Volume:** Cut total sets by **50%** (e.g., 3 sets become 1–2 sets; 2 sets become 1 set).
  * **Intensity:** Back off to **3–4 RIR** across all exercises.
  * **Extenders:** Eliminate all rest-pause, lengthened partials, and loaded stretches.
